About the Province

History & Location

Sultan Kudarat is located in the southwestern portion of Mindanao, bounded by Cotabato, South Cotabato, Sarangani, and Maguindanao. The province was officially established in 1973 and named in honor of the legendary Muslim ruler, Sultan Muhammad Dipatuan Kudarat, who defended the region against foreign rule.

Culture, Traditions & Why Visit

The province serves as a wonderful melting pot of cultures, harmonizing Christian settlers, Muslim Maguindanaons, and diverse indigenous tribes like the Blaan, Tboli, and Manobo. Tourists flock here to experience uncommercialized, pristine eco-tourism, safe community networks, and the proud heritages preserved across generations.
